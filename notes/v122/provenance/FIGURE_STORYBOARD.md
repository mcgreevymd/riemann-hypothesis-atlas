**fig1_two_walls_shioda_tate (front).** "Two walls, one missing H¹: what a class map Φ must cross."
- (a) Determinant wall = product formula **[Exact]**. The chart determinant p^k − 1 grows with k, while the needed atom log p is flat. log(p^k − 1) = Σ v_ℓ log ℓ over ℓ | p^k − 1 with ℓ ≠ p, so the graph meets Δ over other primes and never over p.
- (b) Finite-window Shioda–Tate split **[identity Exact; counts Numerical, n = 256]**. n₋(Q) (bound states of H_A below M_A) and n₋(S) (trivial lattice) are plotted against a common prime-weight scale s. Only s = 1 keeps them equal at A ≥ 1.5. Controls are not Euler data.
- (c) The Riesz lifts of C± behave like the fibre classes f₁, f₂ **[Numerical, mesh-dependent]**: I(H₊,H₊) → 0 and I(H₊,H₋) → 1.
- (d) Near-null = numerically trivial over F_q **[Exact]**: rank 2g, kernel = multiples of L̃ (Cayley–Hamilton). For ζ it is an analogy, and no window has an exact kernel.
- Caption footer: **RH is not proved; Φ is not constructed; no Hodge sign.**
