## Latest results (v1.19)

> **The Riemann Hypothesis is not proved.** Night of 6 Nov 2026, integrated 7 Nov 2026. Structural refinements and consistency tests. No new independent positivity theorem. No ample class, no compatible J, and no metric that reproduces the Weil form were constructed. Rank 1 is unchanged: `kahler_triple_polarization`.

**Analogy.** Weil’s form wants to be the energy of many springs, minus a counterterm. Every spring that the identity writes down is nonnegative. Whether the springs dominate the counterterm for every test function is the open comparison, and it is Weil positivity on the pole annihilator, rewritten. A Galerkin margin that shrinks toward zero shows how tight the balance is. It does not certify the inequality.

**Two groupings, kept apart.** The spring energy ∫ k∞(a) ‖F − T_a F‖² da + Σ a_n ‖F − T_{log n} F‖² is termwise ≥ 0, and M = −K(0) + 2 Σ a_n takes the diagonal, with K(0) = Re ψ(1/4) − log π. After that subtraction, each prime leaves −2 a_n ρ(log n). The autocorrelation ρ can help or hurt. That signed net term is not the spring energy. An anharmonic functional whose Hessian at the origin equals W restates the same form and does not sign it. The archimedean Lévy piece is prior.

**Boundary.** The only Hermitian form on Tate’s two numbers (f(0), ∫ f) invariant under Fourier swap and unitary scaling is hyperbolic of signature (1, 1), or zero. The boundary is not a source of positivity. Globally, E0 is dense in L² and not closed: there is no orthogonal projection that removes the poles on the whole line. On one window the kernel is closed of codimension 2. A in the spring scripts is the full width of [−A/2, A/2]. L in the boundary checks is the half-width of [−L, L]. The bijection by L = ∂² − 1/4 is Connes–Consani 2021, Lemma 3.3, prior. Finite-field samples lie on the same hyperbola and are not Mellin integrals.

**Cousins, not one formula.** A Robertson–Schrödinger Gram, a BPS norm on a Kähler surface, the Hodge index on an algebraic surface over a finite field, and a no-ghost quotient are related architectures. They are not the identity “every mechanism is a square plus an indefinite boundary.” A commutative positive functional can be independent of the zeros. W is unbounded, so the object would be a weight on test functions, not a normalized state on bounded translations. Neither that representation nor the reference form G0 selects W. The pole plane as an II₁,₁ lattice is an analogy. No arithmetic lattice is constructed. Clifford multiplication exists for an indefinite metric. Torsion corrections are convention-dependent. The ratio Λ(1/2 − it)/Λ(1/2 + it) equals 1 by the functional equation, so it has no group delay. The local archimedean delay remains the kernel K.

**The target, at the right strength.** A map into a Hilbert space, or a semidefinite quotient, with squared norm exactly W, is Weil positivity. That existence statement is the Riemann Hypothesis, restated. Four further demands — an independent reason for the norm, scaling and window consistency, a global coupling of Euler factors and Γ data, failure on Davenport–Heilbronn — are criteria for a useful construction. They do not drop out of the Riemann Hypothesis automatically. Place-local squares cannot match the signed local terms one place at a time. Primes, Euler factors, and Γ data remain legitimate zeros-blind inputs. Connes 1999 proves a projection order in characteristic p; the number-field comparison is the open target equivalent to RH. Subtraction alone is not a sign. The sought arithmetic correspondence geometry is not built. The v1.15 transverse space H₊ is this same brief, with that correction. Deninger’s positive leafwise star remains conjectural.

**Numbers, with the certificate rule.** K = 8, exact E0, reproduced: smallest Rayleigh quotient 0.06734 at full width A = 1, and 1.65×10⁻⁸ at A = 2. Positive finite minima are upper bounds, not certificates, and they do not control K → ∞. On a free basis at the same K, W minus the pole term has one negative Rayleigh quotient (about −1.52 and −3.28); those are strong numerical witnesses, not certified eigenvalues, because no rigorous error bound is attached. W’s own Galerkin minimum on that free basis is positive and is not a certificate. The finite-field split D² = 2 b₀ b₁ + D_⊥² was re-checked: inertia, not the determinant, tracks Hasse (151 traces). A trace-norm leakage cannot be negative. The draft test that looked for one was not run.
