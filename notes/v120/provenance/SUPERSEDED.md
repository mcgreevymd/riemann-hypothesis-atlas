# Provenance for atlas v1.20

The directory `bundle/` is the research bundle archived verbatim. It is a historical source. The public atlas does not lead with a formula that `AUDIT.md` or `PUBLICATION_AUDIT_matching1983.md` superseded.

Binding order:

1. `bundle/PUBLICATION_AUDIT_matching1983.md` is the parent math audit. It overrides the raw matching notes and the moment-criterion wording.
2. `bundle/AUDIT.md` overrides earlier scratch wording, rows A1–A24.
3. `bundle/notes/matching1983_notes1983_raw_superseded.md` is a superseded draft. Do not cite it. The corrected note is `matching1983_notes1983.md`, and it remains subordinate to the publication audit.
4. The first storyboard’s name “Hasse wall” for the model null conic is overridden. The aligned bundle and the public figures say “model discriminant conic” or “model null conic Q = 0 / discriminant boundary”.
5. The aligned bundle replaces the loose phrases “certified brackets” and “product-formula defect.” E0 numbers are estimates. M_A is a sum of local means, with no global idele coupling.
6. The animation prints two gauges. Q(h_s) = −4s belongs to the homogeneous representative h_s = (0, 1, s). The affine point drawn at height q = 1/s has normalized value −4/s. The sign, for s > 0, and the projective limit x_∞ are the invariants. The two numbers are not interchangeable.
7. The level set v_T² + 4s v_Q² = 1 tends to a pair of lines. The filled sublevel tends to a strip. The level set is not itself a strip.
8. `INDEPENDENT_MATH_AUDIT.md` is a read of the parallel manuscript. It found no blocking mathematical error. The atlas uses the overlapping corrections. It does not contain the manuscript, and it does not adopt any earlier announcement of a proof of RH.

Local staging that stopped at v1.17 was not copied over v1.19. The public file began at commit `65153b7d93b7314bd3f8359ac1c6c7e6f23fbba1` (1025 concepts, 1990 relations).

The Riemann Hypothesis is not proved. Statements about a thermodynamic or RH-unitarity realization of W are the programme, not an established physical theory.
