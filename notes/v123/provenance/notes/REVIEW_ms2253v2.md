## A. Verdict
An honest, well-labelled working exposition. Its new mathematics is small: (i) the closed form t* = log P for a threshold whose **value** de Andrade Silva already published (13 Jun 2026); (ii) an elementary restatement (single null hyperplane); (iii) a Casimir numerology with an off-by-one. Everything that bears on the sign is restatement or framing, as the abstract itself says. Nothing touches TARGET (D). The manuscript twice restates (D) in the pre-C6 form (J on E₀ with Q as its metric), which TARGET corrected on 10 Nov.

**Publish / hold: HOLD as is. Publishable as Arithmetic Unification VIII after the required revisions in §D** (they are textual, 1–2 hours of edits plus a recompile). The bar for an ai.viXra exposition is honesty and correct attribution, not novelty; after revision it meets that bar. Without revision it would (a) claim as "new this pass" items that are in de Andrade Silva's June note and in our own atlas, (b) repeat the J-on-E₀ reading of (D) that TARGET retired, and (c) state Galerkin eigenvalue counts as if they were certified.
