# Checks t2271–t2286 (Grok Bot as checker; proposals relayed by J.W. McGreevy, unverified until checked)

**RH is not proved.** Each item is classified as [Proved], [Numerical], [Framing], or [Closed] (restatement or closed route).

1. **Maass–Selberg / Witten.** [Closed]
   - The Maass–Selberg norm of truncated Eisenstein series is positive unconditionally (ledger §335). The DH analogue is also positive.
   - Edge versus centre: the unitary axis Re s = ½ of E(z, s) corresponds, via φ = ξ(2s−1)/ξ(2s), to ζ at Re = 1, while ζ's own line sits at Re s = ¼ in that variable. This is the "½ offset" (TARGET C16).
2. **Ruppeiner / Lee–Yang / Fisher.** [Closed]
   - An Euler field is Ruppeiner-flat; the curvature detects non-multiplicativity, and real-β positivity is ghost-blind (L116).
   - ζ's zeros are Fisher zeros, not Lee–Yang zeros (L124).
3. **Photon duality / Hodge star.** [Closed]
   - On a Riemannian 4-manifold, ∫F∧*F = ‖F‖² ≥ 0 and *² = +1 on 2-forms. It is the intersection pairing ∫F∧F that can be indefinite.
   - The earlier statement that ∫F∧*F is indefinite was wrong.
   - The supplied Riemannian metric is not the missing arithmetic sign. C×C is a 4D Riemannian example.
4. **f∗f̃.** [Closed] It is positive-definite (Bochner), but it is not an effective class. Positive-definiteness of the test function is not the sign.
5. **Gravity / rest energy.** [Framing]
   - P is the invariant mass² of the pole plane. E0 = zero 2-momentum. Q plays the role of internal energy.
   - E0 is boost-invariant as a family of spaces (L194(a)), but Q restricted to it is the content.
6. **Degree / conductor in quartet language.** [Framing, exact counts]
   - N(T) ≈ (T/2π)·log(qT^d/(2πe)^d). Degree sets the quartet density; the conductor shifts the resolution scale (log q in the onset law).
   - Classical DH has degree 1 and q = 5. Z₁ and Z₂ have degree 2 and q = 20. L(χ)L(χ̄) has degree 2 and q = 25.
7. **Equivalence principle / windows.** [Framing over exact facts]
   - Prime term n is present iff r > log n (exact).
   - Corrections:

8. **Kepler / perpendicularity (t2286).** [Framing + Closed]
   - (1) The RH-equivalent statement uses the L² complement ⟨F, cosh(u/2)⟩ = 0. With f̂(s) = ∫F(u)e^{(s−½)u}du, f̂(1) = C₊ and f̂(0) = C₋, so this is f̂(0) = −f̂(1); the sign is correct in this convention. Over all windows RH ⇔ Q ≥ 0 on cosh^⊥ (L194(e) 6′), and also ⇔ Q ≥ 0 on E0 (L194(b)).
   - The Q-orthogonal complement {f : Q(cosh, f) = 0}: by Haynsworth, n−(Q|v^{⊥_Q}) = n−(Q) − [Q(v) < 0], assuming Q(v) < 0 and that the restriction is nondegenerate. Under those assumptions, "Q ≥ 0 there" is the index-1 statement n−(Q) ≤ 1, which is implied by RH but not equivalent (L194(e)5). Q(cosh) < 0 is observed but not proved for all r.
   - So the claim is right in substance. Its label "the L194 correction" refers to L194's correction of the h_r reading (Q's ground state), which has the same index content.
   - E0 is neither complement: it is cosh^⊥ ∩ sinh^⊥, codimension 1 inside cosh^⊥. Conflating E0 with the Q-complement would be an error.
   - (2) Dictionary [Framing]: cosh ↔ L, sinh ↔ A (Runge–Lenz), cosh^⊥ ↔ the orbital plane, J = L̂× (the 3D Hodge star of the normal; a free J as in L192). Positivity there is inherited from the Euclidean ambient.
   - Kustaanheimo–Stiefel: fixed negative-energy Kepler motion becomes, after a time change and a constraint, an oscillator on ℂ² with a compatible positive metric. This is an analogue of (D), not an arithmetic realization.
   - With ω = dx∧dy and J multiplication by i, ½ω(z, Jz) > 0, and the Hamiltonian flow is e^{−it} under the convention i_Xω = dH, up to frequency normalization.
   - For E > 0 the oscillator is inverted (a boost).
   - (3) Why it does not transfer [Closed]:
     - There is no canonical J on a complement in infinite dimensions.
     - Q is indefinite (one negative direction from the pole plane), so there is nothing to inherit.
     - ζ's scaling flow is a boost, so "a hidden rotation with positive generator" is Hilbert–Pólya.
   - Prior coverage: TAA L31 (§40, KS/Kepler), L71 (§80, the KS/Hamiltonian lift), L83 (§92, LRL → H³), L161 (§179, apsides/Lagrange; closed, TARGET C14). Atlas: `ks_quaternion_lift`, `moser_orbit_space_s2xs2`, `kepler_so4_contraction`, `lrl_hidden_symmetry_trichotomy`, `ks_lc_preserve_sign`.
   - Verdict: it explains the gap and does not close it.
