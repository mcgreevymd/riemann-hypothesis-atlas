**Headline results**
- **T1 (ζ).** n−(Q|E₀) = 0 at every r ≤ 10 and every N. n−(Q) = 1 and n−(W) = 0 throughout. The minima are Galerkin upper bounds and not converged for r ≥ 2.
- **Control (χ₄).** No negative eigenvalue at any r ≤ 10.
- **T2 (DH).** Index 0 up to r = 3.25, tiny negatives at 3.5, and O(0.1–10) negatives from r = 3.75. These come one per parity per off-line quadruple (85.7, 114.2, 166.5, 176.7) as each becomes resolved.
  - The negative eigenvectors oscillate at those ordinates.
  - The zero-side sum reproduces the arithmetic eigenvalues to about 10⁻⁷, with the off-line terms supplying the negativity.
  - The sharp index-count conjecture is refuted by extra tiny negatives.
- **T3.** Claude's h_r (Q's ground state) is positive for every r (proved: Q is Markov) and is ≈ cosh. Its L² correlation is ≥ 0.993 for r = 1–10 (limited by N), but the numbers in Claude's table are in-span correlations.
  - The Perron vector of W is not cosh: its correlation with cosh falls from 0.97 to 0.12 as r goes from 0.25 to 10.
  - λ₀ ≈ −(r + 2sinh(r/2)) = −λ_max(P) follows exactly from Q = W − P.
  - λ₁ ≥ 0 (Q ≥ 0 on h_r^⊥) is implied by RH but is not equivalent to it. Q ≥ 0 on cosh^⊥ for all r *is* equivalent to RH.
